Privacy Policy

How It Works Uploader · Last updated 31 October 2026

How It Works Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the operator of the How It Works channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It has no other users.

Information the app accesses

  • Authorization tokens issued by Google (YouTube) and TikTok when the operator connects their accounts. They let the app upload videos to those accounts.
  • The channel or account name (YouTube channel title, TikTok display name), read once when an account is connected, to confirm the right account was chosen.

The app does not read comments, messages, analytics, followers, contacts or any data about other people.

How it is used and stored

Tokens are used only to upload videos made by the channel. They are stored encrypted in Amazon Web Services Systems Manager Parameter Store (Jakarta region) and on the app's own server, and are never shared, sold or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions.

TikTok

Uploads to TikTok go through TikTok's Content Posting API and are subject to TikTok's terms and privacy policy. Access can be removed in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking access with Google or TikTok makes the stored tokens useless. To have them deleted from the app's storage, contact the operator below; they are deleted within 7 days.
