How It Works Uploader

How It Works is a channel of short animated videos that explain how everyday things work: brakes, batteries, toilets, turbines.

This uploader is the private tool its operator uses to publish those videos. Each night it uploads the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own YouTube account and sends them as drafts to the channel's own TikTok account, where they are reviewed and posted.

Who can use it

Only the operator of How It Works. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account or data.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the connected channel, and to read the channel's name to confirm the right channel was connected.
  • TikTok: permission to send videos to the connected account's inbox as drafts, and to read the account's display name.

Details are in the privacy policy.
